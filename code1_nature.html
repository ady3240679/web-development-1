The Main Colors of Nature

Nature has beautiful colors. Every color represents a different part of our environment.

1. Four Nature Colors

We can find four primary colors in our world:

  • Green: The color of trees, plants, and forests.
  • Blue: The color of the sky and the oceans.
  • Brown: The color of the earth, soil, and rocks.
  • Yellow: The color of the sun and desert sand.

2. Basic Natural Elements

These elements are important for the planet:

  1. The Sun gives light and warm energy.
  2. The Sky brings rain and clouds.
  3. Trees clean the air and grow.
  4. Soil holds the roots of plants.

3. How to Protect the Environment

We must keep nature clean by following these rules:

  • Do not cut down green trees.
  • Do not throw plastic in the blue oceans.
  • Plant more flowers everywhere.
